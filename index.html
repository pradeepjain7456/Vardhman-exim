About Vardhman Exim

VARDHMAN EXIM is a Kurti Manufacturer & Wholesaler based in Surat, Gujarat, working within one of India's established textile and garment manufacturing hubs.

Our focus is women's ethnic wear, built around premium 3-piece kurta sets — a kurta, a matching bottom and a dupatta, designed together as one complete outfit for the wholesale market.

Every set is made in Cotton 60×60 fabric, available in sizes M to 3XL across 5 colour options, with a wholesale MOQ of 2 sets per design.

Our collections are created for boutiques, retailers, resellers and bulk buyers who need ready-to-sell kurti designs for their stores. Pricing and availability are shared directly over WhatsApp for every enquiry.
